About this calculator. Estimates the value of a DevRev Computer implementation across four benefit categories for directional planning — not a guarantee. All inputs are editable locally; nothing is sent to a server.
  • NPV is calculated over a 3-year horizon at the discount rate set in the header. Year 1 is discounted by (1+r), Year 2 by (1+r)², Year 3 by (1+r)³.
  • Support: salary_savings = (FTE without automation − FTE with automation) × cost per FTE, minus DevRev cost. Setup cost hits Y1 only.
  • Search: sums productivity, collaboration, and decision-quality components, then applies annual adoption minus cost.
  • NPS: treats incremental retention + referral revenue as a recurring annual cash flow, discounted over the horizon.
  • AI centralization: compares annual run-rate of a fragmented AI stack against a single DevRev subscription for the same population.
